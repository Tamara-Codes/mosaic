# Landing Page CRO Analysis for ferros.menu

**Analysis Date:** 2026-02-17
**Page Analyzed:** ferros.menu (LandingPage.tsx)
**Framework Used:** page-cro skill

---

## Page Context
- **Page Type**: Landing page (pre-launch/founder program)
- **Primary Goal**: Get founding partner applications (contact form submissions)
- **Target Audience**: Restaurant owners (Croatian market, tourist-heavy restaurants)

---

## CRO Analysis

### 1. Value Proposition Clarity ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

**Strengths:**
- Clear headline: "AI jelovnik kojim upravljate putem WhatsAppa" (AI menu managed via WhatsApp) - immediately communicates the core differentiator
- Three-part subtitle effectively covers the key benefits: AI translations, custom design, WhatsApp management
- The "This is not just another generic menu" section reinforces differentiation

**Issues:**
- The value prop is feature-focused rather than outcome-focused. You're telling what it IS, not what it DOES FOR THEM (increased revenue, saved time, happier customers)

### 2. Headline Effectiveness ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

**Current:** "AI jelovnik kojim upravljate putem WhatsAppa"

**Strengths:**
- Mentions the unique differentiator (WhatsApp management)
- Includes AI as a modern, credible technology marker

**Weaknesses:**
- Doesn't communicate the primary benefit or outcome
- Missing specificity about results (time saved, revenue increase, problem solved)

### 3. CTA Analysis ⚠️ (Needs Work)

**Primary CTA:** "Postani osnivački partner" (Become a Founding Partner)

**Issues:**
- **CTA copy is weak** - focuses on action, not value
- "Osnivački partner" sounds like work/commitment rather than exclusive opportunity
- You have TWO CTAs competing for attention in hero section (founding partner + demo)
- CTA appears 3 times on page but always with same copy

**Secondary CTA:** "Pogledaj Ferros u akciji" (See Ferros in action)
- Opens a modal with placeholder video - this is friction if video doesn't exist yet

### 4. Visual Hierarchy ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

**Strengths:**
- Clean, scannable design with good use of white space
- Orange accent color draws attention to key elements
- Card-based layout in features/benefits is easy to scan
- Mobile-responsive design

### 5. Trust Signals and Social Proof ⚠️⚠️ (Critical Gap)

**Major Issues:**
- **NO customer logos** (you mention adding partner logos later, but they're not there)
- **NO testimonials** from beta testers or early partners
- **NO case study data** (e.g., "Restaurant X increased orders by 30%")
- **NO review scores**
- Only social proof is "3/5 spots remaining" - but this is **fake scarcity** that could backfire

### 6. Objection Handling ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)

**Excellent FAQ section covers main objections:**
- "What if guests don't know QR codes?" ✓
- "What if waiters think it's complicated?" ✓
- "Do I need new devices?" ✓
- "Is it just Google Translate?" ✓
- "Can I change prices mid-service?" ✓
- "How long does setup take?" ✓

**Missing objections:**
- Pricing concerns (no pricing mentioned at all)
- "What happens after founding partner program ends?"
- "What if I don't like it?" (no refund/guarantee mentioned)
- "How reliable is WhatsApp management?" (what if messages fail?)

### 7. Friction Points ⚠️

**High Friction:**
- **Demo modal has no video** - clicking "See Ferros in action" leads to "Video coming soon" placeholder. This breaks trust.
- **No pricing transparency** - not even a hint of cost range
- **Founding partner benefits unclear** - "without setup costs" waived - but what ARE setup costs normally?
- Contact form asks for mobile number but doesn't explain why (WhatsApp setup?)

**Medium Friction:**
- Navigation has login button - may confuse first-time visitors (am I supposed to have an account?)

---

## Recommendations

### 🎯 Quick Wins (Implement Now)

1. **Remove or complete the demo modal**
   - Option A: Remove "See Ferros in action" button until video exists
   - Option B: Link to actual working demo menu instead of video
   - Current state damages credibility

2. **Improve primary CTA copy**
   - Change from: "Postani osnivački partner"
   - To something benefit-focused (see copy alternatives below)

3. **Add one testimonial/quote** (even if from beta conversation)
   - Place near first CTA in hero section
   - Format: "[Benefit they experienced] - Restaurant Owner Name, Restaurant Name"

4. **Clarify mobile number requirement**
   - Add helper text: "Potrebno za WhatsApp pristup" (Required for WhatsApp access)

5. **Make scarcity real or remove it**
   - If "3/5 spots" is real, add date: "za veljača 2026" (for February 2026)
   - If not real, remove it entirely - fake scarcity backfires

### 🚀 High-Impact Changes (Prioritize)

1. **Reframe headline to be outcome-focused**
   - Current focus: What it is (AI menu)
   - Better focus: What it achieves (more orders, saved time, happier tourists)

2. **Add proof section above contact form**
   - Even without customer logos, add:
     - Founder photos/credentials
     - Technology badges (AI providers you use)
     - "Built by restaurant tech veterans" or similar credibility

3. **Single CTA strategy in hero**
   - Remove secondary "demo" CTA from hero
   - Move demo CTA lower on page (after features)
   - One clear primary action per section

4. **Add pricing transparency**
   - Don't need exact numbers, but give context:
     - "Normalna cijena: €X/mjesec"
     - "Osnivački partneri: €Y/mjesec (doživotno)"
   - Removes biggest objection

5. **Strengthen founding partner value prop**
   - Current: Lists features (no setup cost, feedback, logo)
   - Better: Frame as exclusive club with tangible benefits
   - Add: Lifetime pricing lock, priority support, revenue numbers from other partners

### 🧪 Test Ideas

1. **Hero headline A/B test:**
   - Control: Feature-focused (current)
   - Variant: Outcome-focused ("Povećajte narudžbe od turista za 30% bez dodatnog osoblja")

2. **CTA copy test:**
   - Control: "Postani osnivački partner"
   - Variant 1: "Rezerviraj osnivačku cijenu"
   - Variant 2: "Pridruži se za €X/mjesec (samo 2 mjesta)"

3. **Hero image test:**
   - Control: Phone mockup
   - Variant: Split image (before/after - old menu vs Ferros menu)

4. **Social proof placement:**
   - Test adding trust badge above vs below first CTA

## 📝 Copy Alternatives

### Headline Options:

**Current:** "AI jelovnik kojim upravljate putem WhatsAppa"

**Alternative 1 (Outcome-focused):**
"Turisti naručuju 30% više kada razumiju što jedu"

**Alternative 2 (Problem-focused):**
"Prestanite gubiti goste jer ne razumiju jelovnik"

**Alternative 3 (Specific benefit):**
"Promijenite cijene i sakrij jela za 10 sekundi, preko WhatsAppa"

**Rationale:** All alternatives focus on outcomes (revenue, avoiding loss, time saved) rather than features (AI, WhatsApp). Test which resonates most.

---

### Primary CTA Options:

**Current:** "Postani osnivački partner"

**Alternative 1 (Value-focused):**
"Rezerviraj osnivačku cijenu (2 mjesta)"

**Alternative 2 (Urgency + value):**
"Osigurai doživotni popust"

**Alternative 3 (Free trial angle):**
"Kreni besplatno - plati nakon rezultata"

**Rationale:** These alternatives emphasize what the user GETS (locked-in pricing, discount, free start) rather than what they DO (become a partner).

---

### Benefits Section - Reframe from features to outcomes:

**Current approach:** Feature → Description
**Better approach:** Outcome → How feature achieves it

**Example:**

Current:
> "Jezici bez barijera - Automatski prijevodi na 100+ jezika"

Better:
> "Turisti naručuju više jela - Razumiju sastojke i opise na svom jeziku, naručuju s povjerenjem umjesto 'pokazivanja prstom'"

## Key Takeaway

Your landing page has **excellent design and objection handling**, but lacks **social proof and outcome-focused messaging**. The biggest quick win is fixing the demo modal (broken promise) and improving CTA copy to emphasize value over action. Long-term, add customer testimonials and frame everything around revenue/time outcomes rather than features.
